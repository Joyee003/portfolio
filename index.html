About

I study how machines can perceive emotion in sound. My M.Sc. research fuses time-aligned speech and instrumental tracks to recognise emotion in Bangla recited poetry and music, a setting where existing emotion datasets and models are scarce.

Before research I spent two years in UX design and product leadership. That work taught me to start from how people actually experience a system, and I bring the same approach to designing annotation studies and emotion-aware interfaces.

Multi-Track Audio Fusion for Emotion Recognition Using Machine and Deep Learning Techniques

Joyeta Banerjee, Mohiuddin Ahmad, Tahmida Tabassum

Current research · M.Sc. thesis

Multi-Track Audio Fusion for Emotion Recognition in Bangla Poetry and Instrumental Music

Supervised by Prof. Dr. Mohiuddin Ahmad, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, KUET. Expected completion: December 2026.

In a recited poem the voice carries prosody, while the music around it adds timbre, rhythm and harmony. The thesis asks how much these non-vocal cues add to emotion recognition, using a corpus that pairs Bangla poetic speech with instrumental music for the first time.

  1. RecordStudio poetry and five instruments
  2. AnnotateThree raters, Fleiss' κ ≥ 0.61
  3. AlignDTW alignment, leakage-safe splits
  4. FuseMFCC, spectral and temporal features
  5. ClassifyCNN against classical models
Seed corpus: one recited poem and five instrumental versions of the song "Tumi Robe Nirobe"
